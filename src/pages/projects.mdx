# Official Projects

The open-source projects that make up Zaparoo. Zaparoo Online and Warp are hosted services rather than repositories; they are documented in the [Online docs](/docs/online/).

## Software

### Zaparoo Core {#core}

The service that runs on your gaming device, watches readers, and launches what a token asks for. [Docs](/docs/core/)
