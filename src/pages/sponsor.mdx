## Why some of this costs money

I want to be direct about why this is paid. There are storage and infrastructure bills involved, but the bigger reason is that recurring income lets me spend more time working on Zaparoo. I'm charging for the maintained online convenience here, not taking local Core features away and selling them back.

More on that in the [Warp launch post](/blog/zaparoo-online-cloud-backups-user-api/).

## What your support funds

- **Development time**: new features, bug fixes, and platform support
- **Servers**: zaparoo.org, Zaparoo Online, update hosting, and community services
- **Test hardware**: readers, platforms, and NFC toys to verify compatibility
- **Community**: Discord, documentation, and helping people get set up

## Businesses and venues

If your business or venue uses Zaparoo, sponsoring keeps the software you rely on maintained. For commercial licensing and trademark questions, see [commercial use](/contact/#commercial-and-trademark-use).
