# Contact

## Open source projects

If you need help or have questions about any of the Zaparoo open-source projects, use the community resources first:

- [Zaparoo Docs](/docs/)
- [Zaparoo Discord](https://zaparoo.org/discord)
- [Zaparoo Subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/Zaparoo/)

You can also open an issue in the relevant [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ZaparooProject/).

## Zaparoo App, Warp, and Zaparoo Online

For account, subscription, billing, or Shop order questions, use [zaparoo.com/support](https://zaparoo.com/support) or email [support@zaparoo.com](mailto:support@zaparoo.com). For app bugs, use the community resources above.

## Commercial and trademark use {#commercial-and-trademark-use}

Commercial use of Zaparoo projects is allowed under the existing licenses, as long as the conditions of trademark usage are met. See the [Terms of Use](/terms/) for details. If unsure, email [support@zaparoo.com](mailto:support@zaparoo.com).

Some sub-projects may not allow commercial use. In that case, contact the original author for permission.

To use Zaparoo trademarks, email [trademark@zaparoo.com](mailto:trademark@zaparoo.com).

Building hardware or publishing a game? See [Integrate with Zaparoo](https://zaparoo.com/integrate?utm_source=zaparoo.org&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=partners) or [turn your game into a card](https://zaparoo.com/game-cards?utm_source=zaparoo.org&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=partners).

## Media inquiries

For media inquiries such as review items, email [media@zaparoo.com](mailto:media@zaparoo.com).

## Cryptocurrency tokens

Seen a Zaparoo cryptocurrency? Zaparoo runs no token sales, airdrops, or giveaways. Check the [official token addresses](/tokens/) before interacting with anything claiming to be Zaparoo.
