Hey! It’s been a while since the last community showcase, and there’s a lot to catch up on from our [Discord](https://zaparoo.org/discord) members. This time we’ve got printed cartridges, a custom Cuphead disc, a reader built into an arcade cabinet, holographic cards, and a few ways to keep growing collections organized.

## prestocube

**Featured pick**

<Gallery captions={false} media={[

prestocube designed and printed a box for their card collection, fitting 72 sleeved cards and four dividers inside. They’ve shared the [print files](https://www.printables.com/model/1803189-nfc-card-box-with-divider-and-mister-kun-logo) if you want to make your own.

## Anime0t4ku

<Gallery captions={false} media={[
  { type: "video", poster: "/img/showcase/Anime0t4ku_collectionlauncher_demo_thumb.webp", src: "/img/showcase/Anime0t4ku_collectionlauncher_demo.mp4", width: 848, height: 478, alt: "CollectionLauncher demo showing a Crash Bandicoot game collection on MiSTer" },

]} />

Anime0t4ku made [CollectionLauncher](https://github.com/Anime0t4ku/MiSTer-CollectionLauncher), letting an NFC scan open a custom game collection on MiSTer rather than launch one game. They also shared their physical collection in a red 3D-printed rack, with each game’s title along the spine.

## conehead
