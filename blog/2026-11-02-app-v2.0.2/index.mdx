Zaparoo App v2.0.2 is out now on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zaparoo/id6480331015) and [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=dev.wizzo.tapto), and the App now officially supports iPad.

This is the store release I mentioned in the [v2.0.0 post](/blog/app-v2.0.0#whats-next). The App itself is identical to the v2 that already rolled out as an automatic update, so if you have that, nothing changes for you on iPhone or Android.

{/* truncate */}

What's new is iPad. Until now the App Store listing was iPhone only, so an iPad ran the iPhone version in a small window. v2.0.2 installs at the iPad's native size in portrait or landscape and uses the [tablet layout](/blog/app-v2.0.0#one-app-three-layouts), with the icon rail, side-by-side panels, and full screen mode. iPads don't have NFC, so NFC tags are scanned and written with a reader connected to your Core, but the camera can still scan [QR codes](/docs/tokens/qr-codes/) and [barcodes](/docs/tokens/barcodes/).
