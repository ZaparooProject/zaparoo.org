# Zaparoo Documentation

Zaparoo launches games and media when you scan a [token](./tokens/index.md) (an NFC card, a toy, a QR code, a disc) on a [reader](./readers/index.md) connected to a device running [Zaparoo Core](./core/index.md). The free [Zaparoo App](./app/index.md) writes tokens and manages settings from your phone. Terms you don't recognise are in the [glossary](./glossary.md).

New here? Use the setup guide. Know what you want to do? Try common tasks. Otherwise, browse the sections below.
