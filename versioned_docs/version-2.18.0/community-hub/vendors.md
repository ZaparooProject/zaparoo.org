# Vendors

Anyone is welcome to sell Zaparoo-compatible hardware, and plenty of people do. Vendors come and go, so this page doesn't keep a list. Ask in the [Discord](https://zaparoo.org/discord) to find someone near you selling custom readers and labels, or making them to order.

Official readers, cards, and starter kits are in the <ProductLink href="https://shop.zaparoo.com/" store="shop">Zaparoo Shop</ProductLink>. They are tested with Zaparoo, and purchases fund development.

For generic NFC cards or reader modules, see the suppliers listed on the [NFC readers](../readers/nfc/index.md) and [NFC cards and tags](../tokens/nfc/index.md) pages.
